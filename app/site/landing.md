# SOUNDBORED

SOUNDBORED turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a soundboard: a grid of icons on its screen, a sound on each key, and a web
page that sends your own sounds and icons to it over USB.

![SOUNDBORED's grid of twelve sounds](manual/img/screen-grid.png "SOUNDBORED on the FM-1's screen")

This is version {{VERSION}}. You install it at your own risk, and the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.

- [Install](install/) it from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- The [Manual](manual/) explains the screen, the controls and adding sounds.
- [Download {{PKG}}](firmware/{{PKG}}) to use the command-line installer: `python3 tools/fm1_install.py {{PKG}}`.
- It's built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp, which come from Felucca by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments. It is
  under MPL-2.0.
